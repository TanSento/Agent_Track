# Tesla Model Y vs. Ford Mustang Mach-E for a 100-Car Sales Fleet

## Quick comparison

| Category | Tesla Model Y | Ford Mustang Mach-E |
|---|---|---|
| Typical MSRP | ~$41.6k–$61.6k | ~$37.8k–$59.7k |
| EPA range | ~294–327 miles | ~240–320 miles |
| Fast charging | Up to 250 kW; very strong Supercharger access | DC fast charging; ~32–52 min (10%–80%) depending on trim |
| Cargo | ~29 cu ft behind rear seats; ~75 cu ft max; optional 7-seat | ~29.7 cu ft behind rear seats; ~59.7 cu ft max; small frunk |
| Seats | 5 standard, optional 7 | 5 |

## Fleet fit

**Tesla Model Y**
- Better range and faster charging support for high-utilization drivers.
- Strong charging network advantage if your routes align with Tesla/NACS access.
- More cargo volume, which helps for demo materials, samples, and luggage.
- Typically the stronger choice for minimizing downtime.

**Ford Mustang Mach-E**
- Slightly lower entry price.
- Familiar SUV layout and solid cargo space.
- Good option if you want a more traditional OEM fleet relationship.
- Range and charging performance are good, but less compelling than the Model Y for heavy daily use.

## Recommendation

For a **100-car sales fleet**, choose the **Tesla Model Y** unless your organization has a strong preference for Ford purchasing, service, or internal standardization. The Model Y offers the best mix of range, charging speed, cargo capacity, and operational uptime, which matters most for sales reps who spend long days on the road.

If pricing or supplier diversification is the main priority, the **Mach-E** is the better fallback, but it is not the stronger overall fleet vehicle.
