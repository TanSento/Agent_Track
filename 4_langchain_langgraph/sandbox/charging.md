# Vietnam Public EV Charging Landscape

## How many public charging points are there?
Vietnam’s public EV charging landscape is still relatively small and highly concentrated. A reasonable rough estimate is **around 3,000–4,000 public charging points nationwide**.

The best public benchmark is the V-Green / VinFast network, which has been widely reported as covering all 63 provinces and cities and reaching roughly **3,000 stations and 150,000 charging ports**. Because sources often mix the terms *stations*, *points*, and *ports*, the most defensible reading is that Vietnam has a large national footprint with many individual plugs, but the number of distinct public locations is still in the low thousands. For fleet planning, the key issue is not just scale, but also charger type, site reliability, and corridor coverage.

## Two major networks a fleet could rely on
### V-Green / VinFast
V-Green is Vietnam’s largest and most geographically complete charging network. It operates VinFast’s existing charging assets and continues to expand them, making it the closest thing Vietnam has to a national charging backbone. For a fleet, this is the safest default option if broad geographic coverage is the priority.

### EBOOST
EBOOST is one of the main independent charging networks in Vietnam and a useful second option for fleet redundancy. Public materials and market reporting have cited **roughly 1,900–2,000 charging points** across about **220 locations** nationwide. While much smaller than V-Green, it is one of the few other networks with meaningful national scale.

## Bottom line
For a sales fleet, Vietnam’s public charging market is workable but concentrated. V-Green / VinFast is the clear anchor network, while EBOOST provides a practical second network. Fleet rollout planning should still confirm route-level availability, connector compatibility, and fast-charging access.
