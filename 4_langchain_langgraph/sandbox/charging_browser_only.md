# Vietnam public EV charging landscape

## Rough size of the public charging network
Vietnam does not appear to have a single authoritative public registry that cleanly reports one nationwide count of *public charging points*. Public reporting and operator materials suggest the market is still concentrated and that the total is best described as **on the order of tens of thousands of accessible charging points, likely around 10,000+**, with the vast majority tied to the VinFast/V-Green ecosystem.

A caution on the number: sources often mix **sites**, **chargers**, **connectors/ports**, and **parking bays**, so any headline count should be treated as an approximation rather than a precise fleet-planning benchmark.

## Two major charging networks a fleet could rely on
**V-Green / VinFast** is the dominant national network to evaluate first. It is widely described as the largest EV charging footprint in Vietnam and has the broadest coverage across major cities and intercity routes. For fleet operations, it is the most important network for route planning and depot/backup charging access.

**EBOOST** is a second meaningful network with public charging deployments in urban and commercial locations. It is smaller than the VinFast/V-Green footprint, but it gives fleets another practical option in the largest metro areas and around retail, parking, and destination sites.

## Bottom line
For a sales fleet, Vietnam’s public charging landscape is **usable but still concentrated**: plan around the V-Green/VinFast network as the primary option, and use EBOOST as a secondary operator in key cities. Because public counts are not standardized, confirm availability by **connector type, site uptime, and access rules** before committing to routes or vehicle mix.
